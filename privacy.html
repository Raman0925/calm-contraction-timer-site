Privacy policy

Calm Contraction Timer — last updated 21 November 2026

Calm Contraction Timer ("the app") is an open-source Android app for timing contractions. This policy explains what data the app handles. The short version: your contraction data stays on your phone.

Contraction data

  • Start and end times, intensity and notes you enter are stored only in a database on your phone.
  • The app has no server and no account. The developers never receive your data.
  • Android backup and device-to-device transfer are switched off for this data.
  • The data leaves your phone only when you export a CSV file or PDF report through the Android share menu, or save a backup file (Pro) to a place you choose, such as Google Drive or Downloads. The app never uploads anything itself; the file goes only where you send it, and is then looked after by that app or service. Temporary copies in the app's private cache are removed after each export or backup, on Delete all contractions and at the next app start.
  • A backup file contains your contractions and notes in plain JSON, so keep it private. Restoring it replaces the contractions on the phone after you confirm.
  • The app contains no analytics. Contraction data is never sent over the network or passed to the advertising or purchase services described below; the free version's ads do use the advertising ID, as explained under Advertising.
  • Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Delete all contractions, removes it from your phone.

Settings

Preferences such as theme, pattern setting and the phone numbers you save are stored on your phone only.

Advertising (free version)

The free version shows one banner at the bottom of the History screen, never on the timer. Ads are served by Google AdMob. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA and UK), the app asks for your consent first with Google's consent form, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings → Privacy options. Ads are limited to a general audience (rated G).

AdMob may use your device's advertising ID, IP address and approximate location to serve and measure ads, as described in Google's privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps. You can reset or delete the advertising ID in Android settings. The app does not allow the Android Privacy Sandbox to infer interests (Topics) or measure attribution from its use. Pro users see no ads: buying Pro removes the banner at once, and from the next launch the consent form and the ads SDK are not started at all.

Purchases

The optional one-time Pro unlock is sold through Google Play. Google Play processes the payment; the app only learns whether this Google account owns Pro, and remembers that on your phone. The app never sees your payment details, and no contraction data is involved. Payments are covered by Google Play's own terms and privacy policy.

Notifications

During a session the app shows a notification with a timer and Start/Stop buttons. It is created on your phone and is not sent through any server.

Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed at children.

Changes

If a future version handles data differently, this policy will be updated before that version is released. Changes to this policy are published in this file in the app's public repository, with the date at the top.
